Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

The short version

This site has no accounts, no tracking and no advertising. What you type stays on your own phone or computer until you choose to send it to us on WhatsApp.

What the site keeps on your device

Your browser holds the following in its local storage. It never leaves your device on its own, and clearing your browser data removes all of it.

  • The items in your basket and their quantities.
  • The star ratings you give to items on the menu.
  • Any note you leave in the notes section, with the name you typed.
  • A copy of orders you have prepared, so we can look them up if you ask.
  • A random id for your browser, used only so that rating the same cake twice replaces your first rating instead of adding a second one. It is not linked to you.

What we receive

We receive your order only when you press send inside WhatsApp. At that point we see your name, your phone number, the day you asked for, your order and any note you added. WhatsApp handles that message under its own privacy terms.

If you call us or send an email instead, we keep your number or address for as long as it takes to deal with your order.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies. It loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google servers to fetch the font file.

Removing your information

To clear everything this site has kept on your device, clear the site data in your browser settings. To have a message or an order removed from our phone, ask us on WhatsApp or by email and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions about any of this can go to the email address in the contact section.

Note for the shop owner: this page describes what the site does today. Read it against your local rules, and have a lawyer check it before you take orders in a regulated market.
